Mac App Client

Last updated September 16, 2026

What it is

Kaleb Fowles, who runs No Outcome, uses Mac App Client on his own computer. It lets his AI coding assistant, Claude Code, read and organize the email in Google accounts that belong to him: it flags alerts that need his attention and helps him clear out junk mail.

No Outcome does not offer Mac App Client to anyone else, and no other person's Google account is connected to it.

Privacy policy

This policy covers Mac App Client only.

  • What it can reach. Mac App Client connects only to Google accounts owned by Kaleb Fowles, and only with the permissions he approves when he signs in.
  • Where the data goes. Mac App Client stores its sign-in tokens on his computer. When a task needs a message, it fetches that message from Google and passes it to the AI assistant he uses, Anthropic's Claude, to carry out the task.
  • What it never does. Mac App Client does not sell data, share it with advertisers, or build profiles of anyone.
  • What it keeps. Mac App Client keeps no database of email. Its sign-in tokens last until Kaleb revokes them at myaccount.google.com/connections.
